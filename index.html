Five tabs. Nothing else.

Coach

Invite a client with a code. Plan their week, set their goals, see their workouts.

Eat

Search, barcode or a photo of the label. Snap a photo of your meal for an estimate (Pro). Restaurant menus. Recipes, water, supplements.

Today

Today's plan and your week at a glance. Start a workout in one tap: sets, reps, weight and rest, cardio and timed work, your split and records.

Progress

Weekly count, records, graphs that are pleasing, not just correct.

Me

Your goals and body, Fullset Pro, settings and language. Export your data anytime.

Works with no signal

Your log lives on your phone first. The basement gym, the plane, the cabin: every set is saved. It syncs when you're back.

Fast enough to use between sets

Last workout pre-filled. One tap per set. Rest timer that doesn't get in the way.

Your data is yours

No ads, no tracking, no selling. Export anytime. Delete your account from the app anytime.
