How it works

  1. 1

    Invite

    Tap Invite a client. Send the six-character code. They enter it on their Coach tab.

  2. 2

    Set goals

    Split, sessions per week, calories and macros, water. They see a small Coach label on each.

  3. 3

    Plan the week

    Full sessions: exercises, sets × reps, weight, rest. Meals: targets and recipes. Up to a month ahead.

  4. 4

    Reuse

    Save a session or a week as a plan. Apply it to another client in two taps.

  5. 5

    Watch

    History and Today for every client. When they change something, you see “changed by”.

Questions coaches ask

Does my client need a paid account?

No. Your client signs up free and joins with your code.

Can a client have more than one coach?

One coach at a time. They can leave and join another; their log stays theirs.

What can I see?

Their workouts, Today, goals and meals you assigned. Not their body photos or anything they didn't log in the app.

Can they change what I assigned?

Yes, it's their day. You see “changed by” on the week view.

Spanish?

Both of you pick your own language on Me → Settings.
