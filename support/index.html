Support

Here is how to get help and what to send.

Email us

support@thefullsetapp.com · We answer within a day.

Write to support

Send feedback from the app

Me → Settings → Send feedback. It opens an email with the app's version and your phone model filled in.

Something's wrong

Tell us what you tapped right before, the screen, and your phone model. A screenshot is gold.

Delete your account

In the app: Me → Settings → Account → Delete account. It's immediate: your account, your cloud backup and this phone's data.

Can't sign in? Email support@thefullsetapp.com from your account's email address, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

Copies in encrypted backups expire within 14 weeks.

Common questions

Where do I get the app?

Open Get the app on your phone and tap Download for iPhone or Download for Android. Updating keeps your data.

My workouts disappeared

They haven't. Sign in with the same account (Apple, Google or email). Your log syncs back.

How do I delete my account?

Me → Settings → Account → Delete account. Everything goes, cloud included, immediately. More on deleting.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone first; with an account, backed up to Google Firebase (US). We don't sell it or show ads.

Can I use it in Spanish?

Yes: Me → Settings → Language, or set your phone to Spanish.
