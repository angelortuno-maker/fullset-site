Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Fullset works on your iPhone without one. An account (email, Sign in with Apple or Google) adds a cloud backup, so your logbook comes back if you change or reset your phone.

How do I get my data back on a new iPhone?

Install Fullset, open Profile → Settings → Account and sign in with the same account you backed up from.

How do I import from Strong or Hevy?

Export your workouts as a CSV file from Strong or Hevy. In Fullset, open History → Import (or Profile → Settings → Import from Hevy or Strong), tap Choose file and pick the CSV. Check the preview (how many workouts, and whether the weights are in lb or kg), then tap Import. Sets with no weight or reps, like cardio or timed holds, aren’t imported yet.

How do I cancel Fullset Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Fullset Pro → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or your Fullset account doesn't cancel the subscription.

How do I restore Pro on another device?

Open the Pro screen in Fullset and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. This deletes your account and everything stored with it in our cloud backup. Data on your iPhone stays until you delete the app.

Does Fullset read Apple Health?

Only if you allow it: Fullset can read your body weight and save your workouts to Apple Health. You can change this any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Fullset.
