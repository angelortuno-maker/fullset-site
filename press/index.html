Everything you need to write about Fullset or show it: the facts, a short description, the logo and real screenshots. Questions: support@thefullsetapp.com.

In one line

Your workouts, your meals, your coach, in your language.

In a paragraph

Fullset is a gym app for lifting and food in one place. It logs a set in a couple of taps and works with no signal. Meals go in by search, barcode, a nutrition-label scan or a photo (with Pro). A coach can plan a client's week for free, for both of them. Everything is in English and Spanish.

The facts

iPhone: on the App Store. Android: a test on Google Play, joined from this site.
Free to start. Fullset Pro: $4.99 a month or $34.99 a year (US), with a 7-day free trial.
English and Spanish.
Made by Angel Ortuno.
