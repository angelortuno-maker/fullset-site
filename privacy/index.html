Privacy policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what the Fullset app ("Fullset", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions: fullsetapp79@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Fullset works on your iPhone first. Without an account, your logbook stays on your phone.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Health and fitness data is never used for advertising or data mining.

What we collect, and why

  • Account: your email address, when you create an account with email, Sign in with Apple (which can be a private relay address) or Google. Used to sign you in and back up your data.
  • Your logbook: workouts, sets, routines, your plan, body weight, food log, recipes and goals. Stored on your phone and, if you have an account, in our cloud backup so you can restore it.
  • Photos and clips you add: form photos and clips are stored with your account. Meal photos you choose to analyse are sent to our AI provider (below).
  • Purchases: whether you have Fullset Pro, from Apple through our subscription service. We never see your payment details.
  • Crash reports: technical details about a crash (device model, iOS version, what the app was doing), to fix bugs. They don't include your logbook.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Fullset reads your body weight from Apple Health and writes your finished workouts to it. Health data is used only to show your progress in Fullset. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties except as needed to provide the app (for example, our cloud backup). You can turn access off in the Health app at any time.

The AI meal photo (Fullset Pro, coming later)

The AI meal photo isn't in Fullset yet. Before it launches, this section will name the provider that analyses meal photos and how long it keeps them, and the app will ask for your permission before the first photo. When you use it, Fullset sends only the photo you choose, without its location data, to estimate the foods and amounts, and you review every estimate before anything is logged. You can turn it off in Settings.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase: accounts, cloud backup and photo storage.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status.
  • Sentry: crash reports.
  • An AI provider for the meal photo, named here before that feature launches, and only when you use it.
  • Apple: purchases, Sign in with Apple, Apple Health.
  • Google: Google sign-in, if you use it.

Each processes data only to provide its part of the service.

How long we keep it

We keep your cloud data while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete its data from our cloud backup within 30 days, including backups.

Your choices and rights

  • Use Fullset without an account; nothing but crash reports leaves your phone.
  • Export your workouts at any time: Profile → Settings → Export workouts (CSV).
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Turn off Apple Health access, camera or photo access in iOS Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it: fullsetapp79@gmail.com.

Children

Fullset isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
