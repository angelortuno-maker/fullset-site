Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what Fullset ("Fullset", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions: support@thefullsetapp.com.

The short version

  • You need an account to use Fullset, so your training and food log are backed up and can follow you to a new phone.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. Fullset doesn't use the advertising ID.
  • Your workouts, food, body measurements and anything you type never go into crash reports.
  • A coach sees your training only if you accept their invite, and you can leave at any time.

Your account

You sign in with Apple, Google, or an email and password. We collect your name and email address (with Apple this can be a private relay address) to sign you in, show your name to a coach you accept, and send account emails such as email verification or password reset.

What you log

Your workouts, plans, food log, recipes, water, supplements, goals, body weight and settings are stored on your phone and backed up to our cloud service (Google Firebase, United States). We use them only to run the app for you: your history, progress and records, and restoring them on a new phone.

Coaching

Coaching is optional and starts only when you accept an invite from a coach. You can have one coach at a time. While you are connected, your coach can see your name, your workout history, your Today screen (including its calorie and protein totals), your goals, the sessions, targets and meals they assigned you and whether each was done, missed or changed by you, and your recipes so they can assign them. Your coach can change your goals and plan your workouts and meals up to a month ahead; those items are marked "Coach" and you can still edit them (your coach is told when you do). Your coach cannot see your email, your body-weight log, your Progress graphs, the rest of your food log, or your photos. You can leave your coach at any time in the Coach tab; leaving ends their access the same minute, and the plans they assigned stay with you as your own.

AI meal photo

If you choose to log a meal from a photo (a Fullset Pro feature), the photo is sent to Google's Vertex AI (United States) to estimate what is on the plate. The photo is used only for that estimate and is not stored by us or by Google after the answer comes back; only the foods you accept are saved to your log. Nothing else about you is sent with it.

A nutrition-label or supplement-label photo is read on your phone and then deleted; it is never uploaded. Exercise form photos stay on your phone.

Your phone's health app

The app does not read your phone's health app (Apple Health or Health Connect) and does not write to it. Body weight comes only from what you enter in Fullset.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or an action fails, Sentry receives technical details (device model, system and app version, the screen and action, recent screen names) under a random install ID, never your account, your log or anything you typed. Turn it off: Me → Settings → Share crash reports.

Usage data

If Share usage data is on, the app sends Google Analytics for Firebase anonymous counts of what you use:

  • the screens you open;
  • when you sign up or sign in, and how;
  • when you start or finish a workout, with its day type and a rough size (like "6–10 sets");
  • when you log a meal or water, and how (search, barcode, photo…);
  • when you repeat a day, change the language, open the Pro screen, or start a trial or purchase;
  • coach invites and assignments.

It never includes your name, email, account ID, weights, foods, notes or body data. The counts are tied only to a random ID for this install, which is reset when you turn the switch off. The advertising ID, Google signals and ad personalisation are off, and Google keeps the counts for 14 months. The switch is on by default, except in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland (or if the phone's region is unknown), where it starts off until you turn it on. Change it any time in Me → Settings → Privacy. Turned off, nothing more is sent.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles payment for Fullset Pro, and our subscription service, RevenueCat, tells the app whether you have Pro. RevenueCat links your status to your account's random ID, never your email. We never see your payment details.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode Fullset doesn't know yet, only that barcode is sent to Open Food Facts, a public food database. Food search uses a list built into the app. In Spanish, Search all foods also sends the words you typed, and nothing else, to Open Food Facts.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on thefullsetapp.com, we keep your email address only to send you one email when Fullset opens to everyone. To stop spam sign-ups we keep a scrambled (one-way hashed) version of your network address, never the address itself. To be removed, email support@thefullsetapp.com and we delete your entry within 7 days.

Who processes data for us

Google Firebase (accounts, cloud backup, app integrity checks) · Google Vertex AI (meal photos) · Sentry (crash reports) · Google Analytics for Firebase (usage counts, if you allow them) · RevenueCat (subscription status) · Resend (account emails) · Open Food Facts (barcodes, and Spanish food searches) · Apple and Google (sign-in, purchases). Each processes data only to provide its part of the service, and none may use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your data stays in your cloud backup while you have an account. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted. Usage counts are kept by Google for 14 months. Resend keeps a delivery record of each account email it sends (your address, the subject and whether it arrived) for up to 30 days.

Your choices

  • Export your workouts and weigh-ins at any time: Me → Settings → Export.
  • Delete your account in the app: Me → Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account and everything in our live cloud service is deleted at once, including coach links and assigned plans, your subscription record at RevenueCat is deleted, and the log on your phone is cleared. Copies in our encrypted backups expire within 14 weeks. If you were a coach, the plans you already assigned stay in your clients' accounts as theirs, shown as from "Your former coach", without your name. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Pro subscription; cancel it first in your Apple or Google account settings.
  • Can't sign in? Email support@thefullsetapp.com from your account's address and we delete the account within 7 days (details: thefullsetapp.com/support#delete-account). You can also ask us there for a copy or correction of your data.
  • Turn off crash reports and usage data in Me → Settings → Privacy, and camera or photo access in your phone's settings.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is encrypted at rest by Google. Only you, and a coach you accept, can read your log. The app checks that requests come from the real Fullset app.

Children

Fullset isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
