# fullset-site
Fullset's public website: `/`, `/coaches`, `/support`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, plus `/action/`, the page behind Fullset's account emails. It's served by Firebase Hosting on thefullsetapp.com (MSG-5457; the hosting config is change 217), and by GitHub Pages until the switch: https://angelortuno-maker.github.io/fullset-site/

- **Built from:** the website spec, `docs/design/website-spec.md` in the app repo (the Architect, Oct 14).
- **How it's built:** static HTML and one stylesheet, `styles.css`, with the spec's tokens as CSS variables. `/action/` keeps its own `style.css`.
- **Links:** they're relative, so the same files work at the domain's root and under github.io's `/fullset-site/`.
- **Fonts:** Bebas Neue and Inter, self-hosted as woff2 under `fonts/`, both SIL Open Font License (`fonts/OFL.txt`). The site makes no third-party request.
- **Scripts:** `/action/` calls Firebase's Identity Toolkit with the key the email link carries. `waitlist.js` is no longer loaded: the waitlist form came off the home page in S1 (MSG-5951 W4; `joinWaitlist` stays deployed until a cleanup change). It and `firebase.json` are byte for byte the app repo's tested fixtures (`__tests__/fixtures/site/`); change them there first.
- **Store buttons (change 285; MSG-5951):** every store button is an `a[data-store]`, and `store-links.js` is their one config: `apple` (the App Store page; the button is Apple's official "Download on the App Store" badge, `img/app-store-badge.svg`, unchanged artwork) and `android` ("Join the Android test", our own button, to the Play closed test's opt-in link until Play production, then the Play listing and its badge, WL1). Each button ships hidden (`data-hide-empty`) and shows only once its link is set, so there's never a dead link. `COACH_WEB_URL` in the same file turns on the header's "Coach login" and /coaches' "Open the coach dashboard" (W9); empty keeps both hidden.
- **Phone frames (change 285; re-shot for MSG-5824):** `img/today.png`, `img/eat.png`, `img/coach.png` (the coach's client page) and `img/progress.png` (not on the home page), and their Spanish twins in `img/es/` are real renders of the app at 390×844 @2x, light mode. Today, Eat and Progress are from 2.0.3 (build 32, 7ae6c7d2); the coach frame is still the Nov 2 render from release/2.0.2 59cc3d61 until its 2.0.3 re-shoot (MSG-5951). The hero shows three phones at every width, Today, Eat and Coach (MSG-5830, MSG-5951 W1); the coach frame is also in the coaches band and on `/coaches`. To make them again from a later build: in an app checkout at that SHA, with its `node_modules`, the functions built and a production web export (`EXPO_NO_DOTENV=1 EXPO_PUBLIC_FIREBASE_EMULATOR_HOST=emulators.qa npx expo export --platform web --output-dir dist-qa`), run `tools/screens/render.sh <app checkout> <out dir>`. It seeds a demo account ("Alex") through the app's own screens on the Firebase emulators (a `demo-*` project, nothing real) and writes `frame-{en,es}-{today,eat,progress}.png`; a second test links a coach to a fresh Alex, sets the goals, assigns the week (the journeys' day, since a coach's invite is checked against the real date) and writes `frame-{en,es}-coach.png`. That test assigns Tuesday to Saturday of the journeys' week, and a coach can only assign today or later, so run it on a Monday or a Tuesday (CT). The web build has no bottom inset and clips the tab labels, so the seed adds an iPhone's 34 px inset under the tab bar before each frame. Every frame passes `ready()` first: nothing on screen may be loading (a Skeleton, a spinner, a "Loading…" or bare "…" label) and the frame's own content must be there (Today's type and the week's dots; Eat's three meals; Progress's records; the coach's goals and five assigned days), or the run fails. Each PNG must be 250 KB or less. `img/og.png` is the 1200×630 share image.
