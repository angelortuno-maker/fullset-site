# fullset-site
Fullset's public website: `/`, `/coaches`, `/support`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, plus `/action/`, the page behind Fullset's account emails. It's served by Firebase Hosting on thefullsetapp.com (MSG-5457; the hosting config is change 217), and by GitHub Pages until the switch: https://angelortuno-maker.github.io/fullset-site/

- **Built from:** the website spec, `docs/design/website-spec.md` in the app repo (the Architect, Oct 14).
- **How it's built:** static HTML and one stylesheet, `styles.css`, with the spec's tokens as CSS variables. `/action/` keeps its own `style.css`.
- **Links:** they're relative, so the same files work at the domain's root and under github.io's `/fullset-site/`.
- **Fonts:** Bebas Neue and Inter, self-hosted as woff2 under `fonts/`, both SIL Open Font License (`fonts/OFL.txt`). The site makes no third-party request.
- **Scripts:** `/action/` calls Firebase's Identity Toolkit with the key the email link carries. `waitlist.js` posts the home page's waitlist form to change 217's `joinWaitlist`; it is byte for byte the app repo's tested fixture (`__tests__/fixtures/site/waitlist.js`), as is `firebase.json`. Change them there first.
- **Store buttons (change 285):** every "Download for iPhone" and "Download for Android" button is an `a[data-store]`, and `store-links.js` is their one config: `apple` (the iPhone link, empty until Angel posts it) and `android` (the Play internal-test opt-in). With no link, or no JavaScript, a button stays on its page's `#get` section. The logos are plain (`img/apple.svg`, `img/android.svg`, Simple Icons, CC0); the official App Store and Google Play badges wait for the store release.
- **Phone frames (change 285):** `img/today.png`, `img/eat.png`, `img/coach.png` (the coach's client page) and `img/progress.png` (not on the home page since R3), and their Spanish twins in `img/es/` are real renders of the app at 390×844 @2x, dark mode, from 3daf90e. To make them again from a later build: in an app checkout at that SHA, with its `node_modules`, the functions built and a production web export (`EXPO_NO_DOTENV=1 EXPO_PUBLIC_FIREBASE_EMULATOR_HOST=emulators.qa npx expo export --platform web --output-dir dist-qa`), run `tools/screens/render.sh <app checkout> <out dir>`. It seeds a demo account ("Alex") through the app's own screens on the Firebase emulators (a `demo-*` project, nothing real) and writes `frame-{en,es}-{today,eat,progress}.png`; a second test links a coach to a fresh Alex, sets the goals, assigns the week (the journeys' day, since a coach's invite is checked against the real date) and writes `frame-{en,es}-coach.png`. The web build has no bottom inset and clips the tab labels, so the seed adds an iPhone's 34 px inset under the tab bar before each frame. `img/og.png` is the 1200×630 share image.
